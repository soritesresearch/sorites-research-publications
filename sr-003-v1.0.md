# The Great AI Infrastructure Liquidation

*The buildout can continue while its shareholders lose money. The opportunity begins where commercial progress survives the repricing.*

**Drew Clement | Sorites Research | Foundational Thesis SR-003 | Version 1.0**

**Published November 3, 2026. Price baseline: November 2, 2026 official Canadian closing prices. Research classification: Foundational thesis. Investment recommendation: None.**

## The boom and the collapse

The AI infrastructure boom can be real, capital spending can be enormous, and stocks associated with it can still suffer extraordinary losses. There is no contradiction. A growing industry and a good investment are different propositions.

Our first thesis examined the multiplication of AI agents and computational work. [SR-002, The Bottleneck Premium](/bottleneck-premium), examined the physical constraints that follow: deliverable electricity, electrical equipment, qualified components and construction capacity. This third thesis asks what happens when investors recognize a real industrial transformation but pay the wrong price for its beneficiaries.

The answer may be an opportunity. It may also be a trap.

**Our central prediction is that commercial conversion will distinguish the next winners: contracted assets, delivered equipment and cash generation will matter more than the size of the previous share-price decline.** Over the next two years, we expect a selective recovery, not a return of every former favourite to its high.

The title describes a market dislocation under investigation; it does not claim that every company below has collapsed, that every decline has the same cause, or that a sector-wide liquidation has been measured. Established manufacturers, leveraged developers, quantum technology and subsea robotics are not interchangeable AI exposures.

This paper is published on November 3, after the November 2 baseline. That earlier close was selected by the author before publication. It is a retrospective measurement anchor, not an executable entry price available to readers of this article, a trade record, or evidence that Sorites called the bottom. No November 3 intraday rebound is represented as a return earned by readers.

## 1. Intelligence scales faster than the physical world

Digital services can often be provisioned faster than power plants, substations and factories. Yet every useful computation must run on physical equipment somewhere. When demand expands faster than deliverable supply, value can migrate into infrastructure that once looked ordinary.

This is a mechanism, not a guarantee of returns. Customers can cancel projects. Efficiency can reduce equipment requirements. New capacity can arrive after scarcity has disappeared. A supplier can become strategically important while its shareholders earn disappointing returns because the starting valuation was excessive.

The question is therefore not whether AI will require infrastructure. It is which businesses can turn incremental demand into durable cash flow per share, at a price that leaves room for error.

## 2. Evidence of demand is different from a cheap stock

Hammond Power Solutions (TSX:HPS.A) reported third-quarter 2026 sales of approximately C$446 million, adjusted EBITDA of C$84 million and C$76 million of cash generated from operations. Power Magnetics backlog was 61.1% above the prior-year quarter. The acquisition of AEG contributed to consolidated results; the quarter was its first full quarter of inclusion. These are company-reported results, and adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP measure. They demonstrate operating activity, not an independent valuation conclusion. [HPS, October 29, 2026](https://asia.hammondpowersolutions.com/news/2026/october/Quarter-3-2026-Financial-Results)

Enerflex (TSX:EFX) reported an October 13 award for more than 1 GW of behind-the-meter natural-gas generation equipment, taking its reported backlog in that category above 1.75 GW, with deliveries through 2028. Those are procurement commitments; they are not completed generating capacity or collected free cash flow. The release does not disclose contract value or customer deposits. [Enerflex, October 13, 2026](https://investors.enerflex.com/news-releases/news-release-details/enerflex-announces-major-project-award-and-increases-behind)

Evolution PowerX (TSX:E; formerly Enterprise Group) announced a natural-gas turbine microgrid deployment for a Canadian energy producer's hydraulic-fracturing operations on October 28. This is evidence of an industrial application. It is not evidence of a data-centre contract, and the release does not quantify deployment revenue or incremental earnings. [Evolution PowerX](https://evolutionpowerx.com/news.php?id=33)

Mattr (TSX:MATR) reported increased North American data-centre wire-and-cable sales volumes in its second-quarter results, without separately quantifying that contribution. Hammond Manufacturing (TSX:HMM.A) supplies electrical and electronic enclosures; this paper does not establish a separately disclosed AI or data-centre revenue stream for it. [Mattr Q2 results](https://investors.mattr.com/news/news-details/2026/Mattr-Announces-Second-Quarter-2026-Results-Sequential-Growth-and-Margin-Expansion-Drives-Record-Second-Quarter-Revenue-and-Adjusted-EBITDA-and-Improved-Full-Year-Outlook/default.aspx) · [Hammond Manufacturing](https://www.hammfg.com/)

These distinctions matter. Strong reported sales do not remove acquisition risk, working-capital needs, debt service, cyclicality or valuation risk. A thematic label is not a substitute for a cash-flow model.

## 3. The largest decline may conceal the largest financing problem

### Keel: power access is not a completed data centre

Keel Infrastructure Corp. (TSX:KEEL / Nasdaq:KEEL; formerly Bitfarms) is pursuing HPC infrastructure development. Its August 10 release reported approximately US$819 million of liquidity as of August 7, including US$698 million of unrestricted cash and US$121 million of unencumbered Bitcoin. That is a dated liquidity snapshot, not November 2 cash or cash available for distribution to equity holders. [Keel Q2 release](https://ir.keelinfra.com/news-releases/news-release-details/keel-infrastructure-reports-second-quarter-2026-results)

Its June 30 Form 10-Q reported 616,736,107 common shares at quarter-end and describes US$588 million principal of 2025 convertible notes and US$458 million principal of 2026 convertible notes. Debt principal, accounting carrying value and potential conversion shares are different measures. Restricted cash must not be counted as unrestricted funding. [Keel Form 10-Q, share capital and Note 13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1812477/000181247726000023/keel-20260630.htm)

An investor cannot subtract gross liquidity from market capitalization and call the remainder the price of the sites. Construction, interest, operating losses and liabilities compete for that liquidity. A power allocation, permit application or negotiation must not be treated as an executed tenant lease. We assign no completed-project value or per-megawatt price target here.

### Xanadu: the listed float is not the whole company

Xanadu Quantum Technologies Limited (TSX:XNDU / Nasdaq:XNDU) is a photonic quantum-computing business, with a different commercialization timeline from an electrical manufacturer. Its August 5 prospectus supplement reports, as of August 4, 248,737,921 Class A multiple-voting shares and 55,655,379 Class B subordinate-voting shares: 304,393,300 shares in total. Class A shares convert one-for-one to Class B; both classes participate economically. Multiplying only the listed Class B count by the quote substantially understates total equity capitalization. [Xanadu SEC prospectus supplement, share capital](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2097163/000121390026092648/ea0302974-424b3_xanadu.htm)

The same filing identifies options, warrants, restricted share units and a synthetic at-the-market facility. A resale registration is not itself a new issue of every registered share. Future financing and equity awards can nevertheless dilute owners. The historical share counts above are not certified November 2 fully diluted totals. No quantum-computing price target is offered.

### Kraken and Draganfly: commercial technology with different risks

Kraken Robotics (TSXV:PNG) reported that its June-quarter results excluded Covelya, acquired July 2. Pre-acquisition cash, debt and shares cannot be used as though they were the post-acquisition balance sheet. The combined business must prove integration and cash conversion; subsea defense and commercial demand are not automatically AI data-centre demand. [Kraken Q2 results](https://www.krakenrobotics.com/news-releases/kraken-robotics-reports-q2-2026-financial-results/) · [Existing Sorites Kraken research](/notes/kraken-robotics)

Draganfly (CSE:DPRO / Nasdaq:DPRO) reported 37,148,523 shares outstanding and C$131.9 million cash at June 30. The CSE issuer page inspected November 3 reports 39,142,756 issued shares and 7,351,428 reserved for issuance. Those different dates cannot be mixed into a precise November 2 enterprise value. Reserved issuance is not identical to a treasury-stock diluted count. Cash burn, financing terms and commercialization remain central. [Draganfly Q2 results](https://draganfly.com/press-release/draganfly-announces-record-quarterly-results/) · [CSE issuer record](https://thecse.com/listings/draganfly-inc/)

**The analytical mistake is to treat these businesses as interchangeable because their stocks have fallen.** None of the capital-structure observations above establishes that the shares are cheap.

## 4. The research universe

The frozen operating-evidence universe for this thesis is **HPS.A, HMM.A, MATR, EFX and E**. The contrasting development and technology watchlist is **KEEL, XNDU, PNG and DPRO**. Only these named groups can count toward the predictions below.

The broader investigation also retains HPSS, WEB, CFF, EDY, KDOZ, FORA, ILLM, KNR, CCDS and DIGI as exploratory identifiers from the Sorites watchlist. Inclusion is an invitation to investigate current issuer identity, business economics, solvency, securities and valuation. This paper makes no verified company-specific operating or valuation claim for those additional names. They cannot be substituted into the frozen tests or price cohort.

This is a research universe, not a model portfolio or a list of recommended purchases. A business may have little direct connection to AI infrastructure even if it has interesting assets or technology. No target price, position size or claimed margin of safety is assigned.

For each candidate, the valuation gate is the same: identify every economic share class; reconcile subsequent issuance and split history; inspect options, warrants, convertibles and contingent consideration; distinguish restricted and unrestricted cash; include debt and lease obligations; and estimate remaining capital spending and operating funding needs. Conversion must not be counted twice as both debt and fully issued equity. A weighted-average diluted EPS denominator is not a current fully diluted capitalization.

HPS and Hammond Manufacturing have multiple share classes; quote-service listed-share capitalization is not their whole-company equity value. Hammond Manufacturing's annual filing identified 8,556,000 Class A and 2,778,300 Class B shares as of March 10, 2026. This is historical evidence of the structure, not a claim that the count remained unchanged on November 2. [Hammond Manufacturing annual report](https://www.hammfg.com/files/news/2026/2026-03-10-annual-report-2025.pdf?v=1773437590) · [HPS exchange record](https://money.tmx.com/quote/HPS.A)

We do not publish current EV multiples or claim current fully diluted counts for the universe where a complete, date-matched reconciliation is unavailable. That limitation prevents an unsupported buy-price conclusion; it does not prevent a dated research thesis.

## 6. Dated, falsifiable predictions

**Operating evidence window:** November 4, 2026 through November 2, 2028 inclusive. A source first disclosed on or before November 3 cannot count as a future success. Quarter-end dates alone do not make an already-public fact prospective. Interim checkpoints are May 3 and November 3, 2027; final scoring is due by November 16, 2028 using information public by November 2, 2028.

1. **Supplier breadth.** At least three distinct companies among HPS.A, HMM.A, MATR, EFX and E will each report either (a) at least 10% year-over-year revenue growth in two consecutive reported quarters, with the issuer explicitly identifying data-centre or AI power demand as a contributor in both, or (b) a new binding equipment award explicitly attributed to data-centre demand, with a disclosed value of at least 5% of the issuer's last reported annual revenue. The attributed revenue need not be separately quantified; attribution is qualitative and will be labelled as such. Existing HPS results and Enerflex awards do not count.
2. **Keel conversion.** Keel will disclose an executed third-party HPC/data-centre tenant lease covering at least 50 MW at a named site, plus committed construction financing for the leased phase. Both must be disclosed within the window; negotiations, non-binding letters, municipal approvals and financing aspirations fail the test. A lease signed before publication cannot qualify as new.
3. **Selection discipline.** The fixed equal-initial-capital trio HPS.A/HMM.A/MATR will have a higher cumulative Canadian-dollar price return by the November 2, 2028 close than the fixed equal-initial-capital quartet KEEL/XNDU/PNG/DPRO. This is a prospective comparison of two named groups, not a statistically established factor strategy or a claim that the quartet was mechanically selected as the four largest drawdowns. Price return excludes dividends; cash distributions will be shown separately for both groups.
4. **Returns with breadth.** At least two of HPS.A, HMM.A and MATR will outperform the S&P/TSX Composite price index by at least 10 percentage points over the same interval. The frozen index baseline is 35,801.09, the November 2 close shown by [TMX](https://money.tmx.com/en/). This uses the price index, not a total-return benchmark. Failure is recorded even if operating predictions pass.

For the group comparison, each security receives one unit of starting capital, fractional shares are allowed, and there is no rebalancing, substitution, leverage or reinvestment. Group return is the arithmetic mean of constituent price returns. Splits and consolidations change share quantities rather than economic starting capital. Spin-offs and merger consideration remain attached to the original holding; cash acquisition proceeds remain as non-interest-bearing cash. Cancelled worthless equity is valued at zero. Suspended unresolved securities are reported as unresolved, with a zero-value downside sensitivity; they are never silently dropped. Delistings and failures remain in the record.

Primary evidence means issuer financial statements, regulatory filings, executed-award disclosures or official project records. A single award or project is counted once. Rumours, promotional targets and restatements of old announcements do not qualify. Material source gaps produce “unresolved,” not a manufactured pass. Later additions or methodology changes must have separate dates and cannot rewrite these tests.

## 7. What would prove us wrong?

The strongest opposing explanation is that the market is correctly pricing lower returns on capital: too many projects, expensive financing, customers unable to monetize AI, and equipment capacity expanding just as demand slows. An apparently scarce asset can become stranded before its owner earns an adequate return.

The commercial-selection thesis fails its published return test if the operating trio does not beat the contrasting quartet. It fails the breadth test if fewer than three suppliers meet Prediction 1. The Keel conversion call fails if both contractual and financing conditions are not met by the deadline. There is no substitution of a more successful developer after the fact.

Additional company-level warning signs are dilution faster than per-share cash generation, debt or refinancing costs consuming operating gains, backlog cancellations, persistent working-capital outflows, failed customer commitments and acquired earnings that do not convert to cash. Two consecutive annual periods of negative operating cash flow in at least three of the five operating-evidence names would materially weaken our assumption that the opportunity is spreading into durable supplier economics. Acquisition-driven growth must be identified rather than described as organic.

Efficiency improvements, cheaper alternative architectures and new generation can relieve bottlenecks. Export controls, defense procurement changes, customer concentration, tariffs, permitting, Bitcoin exposure, quantum technical milestones and thin trading can dominate individual outcomes. Small-cap equity can lose all its value. A recovery in an industry does not imply recovery to a former share-price high.

## 8. The opportunity is not the collapse

The question is which business becomes better while its security becomes reasonably priced after all claims on its value are accounted for.

A signed contract is stronger evidence than a slide. Collected cash is stronger evidence than an adjusted earnings headline. Per-share progress is more relevant to owners than growth financed by issuing ever more shares.

The market may have been early in recognizing the transformation and wrong about the valuation. That possibility deserves investigation and a public record. It does not justify buying every casualty.
