# Evidence and valuation appendix

SORITES RESEARCH · SR-004 · The $300 Million Question · November 12, 2026 · Public Version 1.0

Public evidence and model record for the article. USD unless stated. No offer, valuation opinion, mandate or buyer interest is asserted.

### How to read the evidence

**Filed disclosure** means a statement or contract appears in a dated SEC filing. It does not mean the SEC verified the statement or that unaudited figures are audited. **Company report** identifies an issuer’s or research organisation’s own claim. **Calculation** is reproducible arithmetic. **Assumption** is an illustrative input without a verified closing value. **Hypothesis** requires further evidence. **Outstanding** means the proposed use cannot yet be underwritten.

The cutoff is November 12, 2026, America/Vancouver. Period ends, publication dates and access dates differ. A June balance sheet amended in October remains a June balance sheet. Undated live documentation is labelled observed November 12. No post-cutoff event is used. The source ledger below records the primary documents actually inspected; it is not a data-room audit.

### Transaction status and mechanics

GoPro’s September 1 announcement describes $285M aggregate conditional cash and roughly 10% continuing ownership for existing holders. The Parent is Action **Acquisition** LLC and Starman Optical, Inc. is Merger Sub; the November 6 amendment corrects the earlier plural rendering as a scrivener’s error. The proxy describes both transaction entities as formed for the transaction, rather than an established operating business contributed at closing. Planned photonics expansion should not be valued as an already proven business. [S03, S04, S05, S34, S35]

Each eligible old common share receives 0.1 surviving Class A share plus $1.14 cash, subject to adjustment. The preliminary proxy supplies a negative $110M working-capital threshold. Under executed §5.18(d), the last completed fiscal month’s statement is delivered five business days before anticipated closing. The proxy’s abbreviated front-page description is less precise about the measurement date; the executed clause governs this model. [S04, S05]

Cash reduction per old share = max(0, −$110M − contractual NWC) / 219.44M.

|---|---:|---:|

These are calculations, not estimates of actual closing NWC. The contract adjusts its definition for specified debt and leases, including treatment of post-signing borrowings. GAAP current assets minus current liabilities is not automatically the contractual measure.

The agreement’s no-shop, superior-proposal conditions, matching period and contingent fee make a competing approach a negotiated legal process. Five business days’ notice/matching and a shorter reset for material revisions apply. The November 6 amendment extends the outside date from December 31, 2026 to March 31, 2027. $10M is a specified-trigger fee, not a universal exit price. The financing commitment described in the proxy is up to $395M, with available GoPro cash, for closing uses; it is not an operating bridge. The amendment requires reasonable best efforts to extend that commitment through the new outside date but does not establish that the extension was obtained. The January 5 lender repayment date disclosed in the 10-K/A is unchanged. No financing condition does not eliminate closing-condition, liquidity or enforcement risk. [S04, S05, S34, S35]

The proxy’s background records a strategic-review process involving other proposals. Unnamed parties must remain unnamed; none can be identified as OpenAI, NVIDIA, Meta, Alphabet or Microsoft from this record. Its hypothetical wind-down analysis is unaudited and includes multiple intangible assets. Neither its estimated equity proceeds nor its implied IP value is a liquidation floor or a separate patent appraisal. [S05]

### What November 10 actually established

The filing identifies delayed Q3 accounting for financing arrangements, an unremediated control weakness, requested covenant waivers and potential default/cross-default acceleration that could jeopardise merger closing. It supplies preliminary revenue of approximately $96M, gross margin of 12.7% and operating expenses of $60M, and continues the going-concern warning. It does not report that acceleration occurred or that Starman terminated. [S01, S02]

Calculated gross profit is $96M × 12.7% = $12.192M; less $60M operating expenses implies about **$47.808M operating loss**. This is neither a reported final result, net loss, EBITDA nor operating cash burn. Revenue versus the disclosed $163M prior-year comparator falls 41.1%; gross margin falls 22.4 percentage points.

Outstanding: the completed Q3 statements; precise covenants waived or breached; lender remedies actually exercised; subsequent agreements; a definitive proxy and meeting timetable. October restatement-related waivers must not be treated as curing the separate November issues.

### The acquisition model

These cases model a hypothetical cash purchase of **all common shares**, separate from Starman. They are capital-requirement illustrations, not a DCF, market quote, executable offer or probability-weighted forecast. Amounts in millions.

Let E = common consideration; Q = equity-linked cash settlements not in E; D = face debt retired; C = usable excess cash. Conventional model EV = E + Q + D − C. Debt premium/interest P and subsequent funding uses are shown separately, rather than hidden in common equity.

| Input or output | $300M case | $500M case | $1B case | Status |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| E common-share consideration | 300 | 500 | 1,000 | Scenario |
| Warrants | 35 | 35 | 35 | Assumed; reprice at each offer |
| Awards not already in E | 15 | 15 | 15 | Assumed; not a verified entitlement total |
| Q total | 50 | 50 | 50 | Calculation |
| Total equity consideration E + Q | 350 | 550 | 1,050 | Calculation |
| D face debt | 100 | 100 | 100 | Assumed closing balance |
| C usable excess cash | (20) | (20) | (20) | Assumed, after operating reserve |
| Enterprise value | 430 | 630 | 1,130 | Calculation |
| P debt premium / accrued interest | 20 | 20 | 20 | Placeholder, not contract settlement |
| Transaction adviser / legal / diligence fees | 25 | 25 | 25 | Assumed |
| Starman termination fee | 10 | 10 | 10 | Contingent assumption |
| Integration / retention / retooling | 40 | 40 | 40 | Assumed incremental cash |
| Initial working-capital top-up | 25 | 25 | 25 | Assumed incremental requirement |
| 18-month operating cash losses | 100 | 100 | 100 | Assumed, including routine capex |
| Total funding envelope | **650** | **850** | **1,350** | Calculation |

Total funding = E + Q + D − C + P + fees + termination + integration + working capital + operating losses.

The model retires existing principal. A buyer assuming some debt must move that amount into continuing financing and model its servicing; it cannot count both retirement and assumption. Cash is netted only if actually unrestricted, transferable and surplus to the continuing business’s minimum needs. If no excess cash exists, set C to zero.

Operating leases are excluded from D/EV and retained in operating losses and subsequent cash flows. A lease-adjusted EV would need corresponding lease-adjusted earnings/cash flows. Working-capital funding is additional to the target’s ordinary operating deficit; losses must exclude that same initial balance rebuild and the separately shown integration expense. Reconcile a monthly cash-flow model before committing funds. No separate tax-benefit credit, patent-sale proceeds, synergies, litigation recoveries or asset disposals are assumed.

The constant $35M warrants and $15M awards permit comparison; they are not valuations. Higher common prices may increase settlements. Deferred consideration, stock consideration or an earnout changes payment timing and risk but remains economic consideration. An optional future carveout is not costless financing.

Seasonality, restructuring and Q3 deterioration make that comparison a warning about uncertainty, not a forecast.

**Financing example:** hypothetical new borrowing of $200M at 10% annual cash interest for 18 months adds $30M interest plus an assumed 2% origination fee ($4M). Keeping principal outstanding, buyer cash/equity funding becomes total envelope − $200M + $34M: $484M / $684M / $1.184B. The acquisition still uses $650M / $850M / $1.35B before those financing costs, and $200M remains owed. This is not evidence that the target supports that leverage, lenders would offer it, or any strategic buyer would borrow. Interest tax benefits are omitted. Avoid adding buyer interest again if later cash-loss forecasts include it.

### Reverse underwriting rather than a price target

Assume the entire unlevered envelope is paid at time zero. Assume ten equal annual free-cash-flow benefits at years 2.5 through 11.5, after an 18-month ramp; discount at 12%; no terminal value. This upfront treatment simplifies the timing of actual future losses. The ten-year annuity factor is 5.650223. Discounting it for an additional 18 months means dividing by 1.185297, giving a present-value factor of 4.766928.

Required annual FCF = (funding envelope − separately justified legacy/residual NPV) / 4.766928.

| Case | No legacy/residual NPV credit | With a hypothetical $100M credit |
|---|---:|---:|
| $300M common | $136.36M annually | $115.38M annually |
| $500M common | $178.31M annually | $157.33M annually |
| $1B common | $283.20M annually | $262.22M annually |

This does not predict data revenue or assert that these values are attainable. The credit must be independently justified, net of the costs already modelled. A patent, brand, subscriber or distribution valuation cannot be added if its cash flows already sit inside the legacy-business or data forecast. A proper buyer model would use monthly funding, scenario probabilities, product-specific cash flows, taxes and explicit terminal assumptions. Negative current earnings do not support an ordinary positive EBITDA acquisition multiple.

### Build versus buy

An acquisition must be compared with the least expensive feasible way to obtain the buyer’s required capability. The alternatives differ in scope; buying a data license does not recreate a global camera company.

| Route | What it can obtain | Principal limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Existing-content license | Fast, reversible rights and utility test | Nonexclusivity; existing data may miss tasks or sensing |
| Commissioned capture with GoPro | Devices, workflow support and new contributor recruitment | Needs task design, permissions, QA and possible contract consent |
| Greenfield / specialist operator | Purpose-built sensing and task coverage | Recruitment, integration and delivery-time risk |
| Minority capital plus offtake | Staged exposure and delivery commitment | No full control; creditor/merger constraints |
| IP / team carveout | Selected engineering and claims | Assignability, liens, retention and stranded service costs |
| Whole company | Roadmap, brand, teams and customer relationships | Acquires operating burden, claims and integration risk |
| Restructuring asset sale | Potentially narrower assets and approved claim treatment | Court/creditor process, competing bids and continuity risk |

**Illustrative commissioned-build alternative:** 10,000 contributors each record 100 hours, producing 1M raw hours. Hardware/kit allowance $500/person = $5M; contributor incentives $12/raw hour = $12M; logistics/ingestion/support $8/raw hour = $8M; recruiting/platform/rights overhead = $5M; annotation/QA $20 per accepted hour. These are analyst assumptions, not supplier quotes, observed unit costs or promised schedules.

| Accepted share of raw hours | Accepted hours | Total program cost | Cost per accepted hour |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 10% | 100,000 | $32M | $320 |
| 25% | 250,000 | $35M | $140 |
| 50% | 500,000 | $40M | $80 |

Cost = $30M raw/fixed allowances + $20 × accepted hours. Acceptance requires deduplication, legal eligibility, technical quality and the buyer’s task specification. This program excludes the price of GoPro’s brand, existing IP and software business. It also excludes custom hardware R&D beyond the assumed kit, buyer model-training compute and robot deployment infrastructure; those costs must be added if required by the specification. Compare both alternatives on the same delivered capability and timetable. Request priced bids from GoPro and at least two operators before asserting savings.

### IP, software and dataset diligence

The detailed year-end filing lists 1,608 issued U.S. patents and 868 corresponding issued foreign patents, with 280 U.S. and 46 foreign applications. The September press release’s “more than 2,500 U.S. patents” does not reconcile to that dated list. Worldwide issued rights are not unique inventions. Require a current family/assignment/maintenance register before making a count or value claim. [S07, S03]

Q1 included a $1.2M sale/licensing IP gain; the public disclosure reviewed does not identify the affected families or retained rights. The ITC’s February determination found no violation on five utility patents while finding a design-patent violation. Neither outcome establishes total portfolio value. Reconcile sold/licensed rights, liens, challenges, remaining life and buyer-specific claim charts. [S08, S17]

Quik, Reframe, cloud uploads and telemetry may reduce the time required to build a capture workflow. Their acquisition value depends on code ownership, developer retention, licences, dependencies, cloud contracts, security, cost to serve, subscription retention and integration. Subscription revenue must fund ongoing storage/support; it is not all royalty-like margin. [S07, S18]

The program permits participating content to be sublicensed and assigned, with contributor warranties about third-party permissions, but actual chain of title remains a diligence task. General participation is adult and U.S./territory based, with modified employee provisions. Audit contract versions, content IDs, acceptance records, scope, prior licences and termination histories. Reconcile the perpetual-license language with limits on new uses after withdrawal. Privacy notices alone do not create training rights. [S14, S15]

For every sample require original bytes and hashes, device/firmware and camera mode, raw-versus-transformed versions, timebase and sensor synchronisation, intrinsics/extrinsics where needed, dropped-frame/clock records, task boundaries, action/pose information, outcome labels and quality flags. Stabilisation, wide-angle geometry, rolling shutter, occlusion, low light and motion blur can matter differently by task. Assess battery/thermal interruptions and upload selection bias. Do not infer depth, force, joint commands, hand articulation or contact from a camera IMU. Match human observations to the robot embodiment and test domain transfer rather than assuming it.

Rights review must cover wearer, employer, site/property, visible people, speech, third-party screens/content, children, sensitive locations and permitted model uses. Worker consent alone may be insufficient. Exclude unresolved material before sample transfer; minimise location and identity data, preserve permitted pseudonymous joins, and test deletion/retention obligations. A participant warranty or an acquirer’s corporate ownership is not a completed permissions audit.

### Prospective tests and falsification

Baseline is November 12, 2026. Formal reassessment is February 10, 2027, 90 calendar days later. No pre-baseline announcement counts as confirmation. The following is a proposed protocol; the buyer’s technical and legal leads must approve it before samples or results are selected.

1. Specify six manipulation jobs and the deployment conditions. Possible jobs include bin picking, connector insertion, fastener use, cable routing, package placement and error recovery. These are proposed tasks, not data already collected or available through Archive Signal.
2. Pre-register baseline, GoPro-increment and alternative-data models, matched compute and annotation budgets, a primary metric, minimum economically useful improvement and uncertainty method. Hold out sites, people and objects to limit leakage. Publish failures to the internal reviewer, not only successful demonstrations.
3. Audit the rights manifest before any transfer. Require no unresolved rights exception in accepted material. Failure stops that sample stream; it cannot be overcome by better model performance.
4. Measure rights-cleared accepted hours/episodes, rejection reasons, effective cost, delivered time, contributor retention and ability to recollect a failed task. Assess incremental performance on unseen tasks and confidence intervals; a larger archive alone does not pass.
5. Require a costed build/license comparison and an executable transaction plan with payoff letters, security settlements, staff retention and monthly liquidity. Approve ownership only if it creates incremental risk-adjusted value over that alternative.

Reject the whole-company thesis if rights cannot support the use, matched evaluation fails the pre-registered useful-improvement threshold, a narrower contract achieves the same benefit at better economics, critical IP/staff cannot transfer, or funding/consents exceed the buyer’s approved limits. Lack of access or missing evidence is **unproven**, not confirmation or empirical falsification. Log separately whether any post-baseline named buyer publicly discloses a capture acquisition, licence or deployment; an announcement is sector evidence, not proof that GoPro is a good purchase or that its shares will perform.
